# Mazand Mall
## Project Timeline & Deliverables
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Project Overview

Mazand Mall is a modern multi-tier e-commerce platform designed to serve both retail customers (B2C) and business partners (B2B), with advanced pricing, inventory, and administration capabilities.

The project will be delivered using a milestone-based approach, allowing the client to review, test, and approve each phase before development continues.

**Estimated Duration:** 14–16 Weeks (3–4 Months)

---

# Project Delivery Approach

The project is divided into three major milestones.

At the end of each milestone:

- Functional software will be delivered.
- A demonstration session will be held.
- Feedback will be collected.
- Approved features will be considered complete before proceeding to the next milestone.

---

# Milestone 1 — Platform Foundation
**Duration:** Weeks 1–5

## Objective

Establish the technical foundation and provide administrators with the tools required to manage the platform.

## Deliverables

### Authentication & User Management

- Customer registration and login
- Partner registration portal
- Mobile verification (OTP)
- Role-based authentication
- User profile management

### Partner Management

- Business application form
- Business document upload
- Manual approval workflow
- Partner status management
- Tier assignment (Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3)

### Administration

- Admin dashboard
- Operator dashboard
- User management
- Role and permission management

### Product Management

- Category management
- Brand management
- Product CRUD
- Product specifications
- Product images
- Product video support
- Automatic image watermarking

### Inventory

- Stock management
- Product availability
- Stock status

### Initial Integrations

- Import products and categories from Holo (initial migration)

## Deliverable Summary

At the end of this milestone, administrators will be able to fully manage products, inventory, users, and partner registrations through a functional administration panel.

# Client Responsibilities

To maintain the project schedule, the client agrees to provide:

- Company branding assets (logos, colors, fonts)
- Product data and media (where applicable)
- Business rules and pricing policies
- Access to required third-party services
- Timely review and approval of milestone deliverables
- Feedback within five (5) business days of each milestone delivery

Delays in approvals or required content may affect the project timeline.

---

# Acceptance Process

Each milestone will follow the process below:

1. Feature implementation
2. Internal testing
3. Demonstration to the client
4. Client review period
5. Feedback and revisions (within the agreed project scope)
6. Client approval
7. Commencement of the next milestone

---

# Out of Scope

The following items are not included unless explicitly agreed upon:

- Marketplace for third-party sellers
- Mobile applications (Android/iOS)
- Installment payment services (e.g., SnappPay)
- AI-powered recommendations
- Loyalty and rewards programs
- Multi-language support
- Multi-currency support
- Advanced ERP integrations beyond the agreed Holo migration
- Custom third-party integrations not listed in this document

These items may be implemented in future project phases.
