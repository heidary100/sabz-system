# Sabz System Platform
# Project Milestones

Version: 1.0

---

# Milestone 1 — Platform Foundation
**Duration:** Weeks 1–5

## Objective

Establish the technical foundation and provide administrators with the tools required to manage the platform.

## Key Deliverables

- Authentication & User Management (registration, login, OTP, roles)
- Partner Management (application, verification, tier assignment)
- Administration (dashboards, user/role management)
- Product Management (categories, brands, CRUD, media, watermarking)
- Inventory (stock management, availability, status)

---

# Milestone 2 — Marketplace & Sales
**Duration:** Weeks 6–10

## Objective

Launch the customer-facing online store with complete shopping capabilities.

## Key Deliverables

- Storefront (homepage, listings, details, browsing)
- Search & Filtering (product search, category/brand/price filters, sorting)
- Shopping Experience (cart, wishlist, checkout)
- Pricing Engine (retail, tier-based, bulk, dynamic, RFQ)
- Orders (placement, history, tracking, invoicing)
- Payments (online payment gateway)
- Content (blog, SEO pages)
- Torob marketplace integration

---

# Milestone 3 — Optimization & Production Launch
**Duration:** Weeks 11–16

## Objective

Prepare the platform for production by completing advanced features, integrations, optimization, and quality assurance.

## Key Deliverables

- Logistics (shipping, Post/Tipax integration, tracking)
- Returns (request workflow, approval, status tracking)
- Notifications (order, partner, admin alerts)
- Performance (optimization, caching, image optimization, DB tuning)
- Security (hardening, permission validation, audit logging, backups)
- Quality Assurance (bug fixes, cross-browser, mobile, UAT)
- Deployment (production deployment, monitoring, admin training)

---
