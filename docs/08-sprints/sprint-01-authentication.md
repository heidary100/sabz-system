# Sabz System Platform
# Sprint 01 — Authentication & Core Features

Version: 1.0

---

# Sprint Goal

Complete the authentication system, user management, partner management workflows, and begin product catalog and inventory foundations.

---

# Duration

Weeks 3–5

---

# Dependencies

- Sprint 00 (Foundation) must be complete.

---

# Scope

## Must Have

- [ ] Partner document upload (business license, national card)
- [ ] Partner verification workflow (operator review)
- [ ] Partner tier assignment
- [ ] Category management (CRUD, tree structure)
- [ ] Brand management (CRUD)
- [ ] Product CRUD (create, read, update, delete)
- [ ] Product media upload with watermarking
- [ ] Product specifications (key-value attributes)
- [ ] Inventory management (stock levels, status)
- [ ] Admin product management views
- [ ] Admin partner management views
- [ ] Holo data import (initial migration)

## Should Have

- [ ] Product search (basic)
- [ ] Stock alerts for low inventory
- [ ] Partner notification on approval/rejection

## Won't Have (This Sprint)

- [ ] Shopping cart
- [ ] Checkout
- [ ] Payment processing
- [ ] Order management
- [ ] Storefront product browsing

---

# Acceptance Criteria

1. Operators can review and approve/reject partner applications.
2. Approved partners are assigned a tier and see tier-based pricing.
3. Admins can manage categories and brands.
4. Admins can create, edit, and publish products with media.
5. Product images are automatically watermarked.
6. Inventory stock levels are tracked and visible to admins.
7. Holo product data is successfully imported.
8. Partner approval/rejection triggers SMS notification.

---

# Notes

- This sprint completes **Milestone 1** deliverables.
- The storefront product browsing and shopping features begin in Sprint 02 (Milestone 2).
