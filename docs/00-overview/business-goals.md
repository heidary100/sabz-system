# Target Audience

## Retail Customers (B2C)

Individuals purchasing electronics and accessories for personal use.

## Business Partners (B2B)

- Distributors
- Wholesalers
- Retail Shops
- System Integrators
- Corporate Buyers

---

# Business Objectives

- Increase online sales
- Expand regional market reach
- Reduce manual order processing
- Digitize partner management
- Improve customer experience
- Increase repeat purchases
- Improve inventory visibility
- Build a strong SEO presence
- Enable future marketplace expansion

---

# Success Metrics

The project will be considered successful when:

- Customers can complete purchases online
- Business partners receive automated tier pricing
- Administrators manage products and orders efficiently
- Product information is searchable and SEO-friendly
- The platform is stable, secure, and ready for growth
