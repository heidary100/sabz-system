# Sabz System Platform
# Product Roadmap

Version: 1.0

---

# Phase 1 — Foundation

Objective: Build the operational backbone of the platform.

Major Initiatives:

- Authentication
- User Management
- Partner Registration
- Product Catalog
- Inventory Management
- Administration Panel

Expected Outcome:

The business can manage products, users, and partners through a centralized administration system.

---

# Phase 2 — Commerce

Objective: Launch customer-facing sales functionality.

Major Initiatives:

- Storefront
- Search
- Shopping Cart
- Checkout
- Payments
- Orders
- Pricing Engine
- Blog
- SEO

Expected Outcome:

Customers can browse products and complete purchases online.

---

# Phase 3 — Production Readiness

Objective: Prepare the platform for public launch.

Major Initiatives:

- Shipping
- Returns
- Notifications
- Performance Optimization
- Security Hardening
- Monitoring
- Production Deployment

Expected Outcome:

The platform is stable, secure, optimized, and ready for production use.

---

# Future Roadmap

Future enhancements may include:

- Native Android application

- SnappPay integration
- Loyalty program
- Referral program
- Gift cards
- AI-powered product recommendations
- Customer support ticketing
- Advanced reporting and analytics
- Multi-language support
