# Feature Specification – Pricing Engine

Version: 1.0

Module ID: PRICE-001

Status: Approved for Development

Milestone: 1 (Core), Milestone 2 (Advanced Rules), Milestone 3 (Promotions)

Priority: Critical (P1)

---

# 1. Purpose

The Pricing Engine is responsible for calculating the final selling price of products for all customer types.

It supports:

- Retail (B2C) pricing
- Partner tier pricing
- Category pricing rules
- Product-specific pricing
- Bulk discounts
- Promotional pricing
- RFQ (Request for Quote)
- Future coupon and loyalty discounts

The engine ensures pricing consistency across the storefront, admin panel, APIs, and future mobile applications.

---

# 2. Goals

- Centralize all pricing logic
- Prevent inconsistent pricing
- Support flexible business rules
- Allow pricing changes without code deployments
- Keep a full audit trail of price changes

# 4. Business Rules

### PRICE-001

Every sellable product must have a base price.

---

### PRICE-002

Guests and retail customers always see retail pricing.

---

### PRICE-003

Approved partners see prices based on their assigned tier.

---

### PRICE-004

Pricing is calculated dynamically and must not be hardcoded into the product.

---

### PRICE-005

The final calculated price must never be negative.

---

### PRICE-006

Bulk discounts are evaluated after tier pricing.

---

### PRICE-007

Coupons are evaluated after all pricing rules.

---

### PRICE-008

RFQ-enabled products cannot be purchased directly until a quotation has been accepted.

---

### PRICE-009

Every pricing modification must be recorded in the audit log.

# 6. Pricing Models

## Retail Price

Default selling price for B2C customers.

---

## Tier Pricing

Tier 3

Retailer discount

↓

Tier 2

Wholesaler discount

↓

Tier 1

Distributor pricing (floor price)

Tier percentages or fixed values shall be configurable through the administration panel.

---

## Category Pricing Rules

Rules may be configured per category.

Examples

Storage Devices

- Cost + Fixed Markup

Accessories

- Cost + Percentage

Printers

- Fixed Selling Price

---

## Product Overrides

Specific products may override category pricing.

Examples

- Promotional launch price
- Clearance price
- Exclusive supplier agreement

---

## Bulk Discounts

The system shall support quantity-based pricing.

Example

| Quantity | Discount |
|----------|----------|
| 1–9 | None |
| 10–49 | 3% |
| 50–99 | 7% |
| 100+ | 10% |

Rules shall be configurable.

---

## RFQ Products

Products marked as RFQ:

- Display "Request Quote" instead of "Add to Cart"
- Allow partners to submit quantity requests
- Generate quotation records
- Expire quotations after a configurable period
- Prevent checkout until a quotation is accepted

---

## Promotional Pricing

Supports:

- Percentage discount
- Fixed amount discount
- Flash sale
- Scheduled promotion
- Category promotion
- Brand promotion

Promotions have:

- Start date
- End date
- Usage conditions
- Status

---

## Coupons (Future)

Supported coupon types:

- Percentage
- Fixed amount
- Free shipping
- Partner-exclusive
- Minimum order value
- Single-use

# 7. User Stories

### PRICE-US-001

As a retail customer,

I want to see the standard selling price,

so I know how much I will pay.

Acceptance Criteria

- Retail price displayed.
- Price formatted correctly.
- Discounts shown where applicable.

---

### PRICE-US-002

As a verified partner,

I want to automatically receive pricing based on my tier,

so I do not need to contact sales.

Acceptance Criteria

- Tier pricing applied automatically.
- Correct tier displayed.
- Retail price hidden if business policy requires.

---

### PRICE-US-003

As a wholesaler,

I want bulk discounts applied automatically,

so larger purchases receive better pricing.

Acceptance Criteria

- Quantity thresholds evaluated.
- Correct discount applied.
- Order total recalculated immediately.

---

### PRICE-US-004

As an administrator,

I want to configure pricing rules,

so business pricing can change without software updates.

Acceptance Criteria

- Rules are editable.
- Changes are effective immediately or from a scheduled date.
- Audit records created.

---

### PRICE-US-005

As a partner,

I want to request a quotation for volatile products,

so I receive the latest market price before ordering.

Acceptance Criteria

- RFQ request submitted.
- Sales team notified.
- Customer receives quotation.
- Accepted quotation can be converted into an order.

# 9. Validation Rules

Base Price

- Required
- Greater than zero

Discount

- Must not exceed configured maximum

Promotion Dates

- Start date before end date

Bulk Threshold

- Quantity greater than zero

Coupon Code

- Unique

RFQ Expiration

- Must be greater than current date
