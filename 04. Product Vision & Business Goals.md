# Mazand Mall
# Product Vision & Business Goals

Version: 1.0

---

# Vision

Mazand Mall aims to become the leading regional electronics marketplace by connecting retail customers and business partners through a unified, modern, and scalable e-commerce platform.

The platform will provide transparent pricing, efficient inventory management, fast order processing, and a superior shopping experience while supporting the unique requirements of wholesale and retail sales.

---

# Mission

To simplify the buying and selling of electronics by providing a trusted online platform that combines the advantages of traditional retail with modern digital commerce.

---

# Target Audience

## Retail Customers (B2C)

Individuals purchasing electronics and accessories for personal use.

## Business Partners (B2B)

- Distributors
- Wholesalers
- Retail Shops
- System Integrators
- Corporate Buyers

---

# Business Objectives

- Increase online sales.
- Expand regional market reach.
- Reduce manual order processing.
- Digitize partner management.
- Improve customer experience.
- Increase repeat purchases.
- Improve inventory visibility.
- Build a strong SEO presence.
- Enable future marketplace expansion.

---

# Success Metrics

The project will be considered successful when:

- Customers can complete purchases online.
- Business partners receive automated tier pricing.
- Administrators manage products and orders efficiently.
- Product information is searchable and SEO-friendly.
- The platform is stable, secure, and ready for growth.
